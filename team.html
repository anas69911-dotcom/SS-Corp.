Anas Ahmed

Founder & CEO

Anas founded S.S Corporation in 1998 after watching hospitals expand rapidly around the world and recognizing the growing need for a reliable link between international pharmaceutical manufacturers and the healthcare systems that depend on them.

That focus on keeping hospitals reliably supplied has shaped how the company operates ever since, on both the distribution and export side of the business.
