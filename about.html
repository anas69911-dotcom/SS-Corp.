About us

A distribution house built over two decades

Since 1998, S.S Corporation has worked as a link between major international pharmaceutical manufacturers and the Pakistani market — bringing established, trusted brands to local pharmacies and healthcare providers.

Alongside that, we export medicine manufactured by local Pakistani pharmaceutical companies to buyers across the world, helping domestic manufacturers reach international markets.

The company is based in Karachi and is run today by founder Anas Ahmed, who started the business after seeing hospitals expand rapidly around the world and recognizing the need for a reliable supply link.
